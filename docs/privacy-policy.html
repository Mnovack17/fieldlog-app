Field Log Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Overview

Field Log is an internal daily-reporting app built for Direct Services Group (DSG) field technicians and supervisors. It lets technicians record daily site-progress entries — including notes, status updates, and photos — that are stored for DSG's internal record-keeping and sent to DSG management by email. Field Log is a workforce tool for DSG's own technicians and staff, not a public consumer app.

Information We Collect

When a technician submits an entry, Field Log collects:

  • Entry details: date, team name, job site, supervisor name, estimated duration, project status, and a description of tasks completed
  • Issue reports: if an issue is flagged, its severity, a description, and (if applicable) a follow-up owner's name
  • Notes: any additional free-text notes the technician enters
  • Photos: one or more site photos attached to the entry

Field Log does not require technicians to create a personal account or provide a name, email address, or password to use the app.

How We Use This Information

Submitted entries and photos are stored in DSG's private database and used solely for internal record-keeping, project tracking, and safety follow-up. Each submission also triggers an automatic email notification to DSG management summarizing the entry, including links to any attached photos. We do not sell, rent, or share this information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes, and we do not use it to track you across other apps or websites.

Third-Party Services We Use

Field Log relies on a small number of service providers to operate:

  • Google Firebase (Firestore database and anonymous authentication) — stores submitted entries and assigns each device an anonymous identifier, with no personal sign-in required
  • Cloudinary — hosts photos attached to entries; each photo is assigned a private URL
  • Web3Forms — delivers the email notification sent to DSG management when an entry is submitted

Each of these providers processes data only as needed to provide their service to Field Log. None of them are used for advertising.

Data Retention and Deletion

Entries and photos are retained for as long as needed for DSG's internal record-keeping and project history. If you are a DSG technician and would like a specific entry or photo removed, contact us using the information below.

Camera and Photo Library Access

Field Log requests camera and photo library access solely so technicians can attach site photos directly to their daily entries. Photos are only accessed when a technician chooses to take or select one — Field Log does not access your camera roll in the background.

Children's Privacy

Field Log is a workforce tool intended for use by DSG's adult technicians and staff. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app or our practices. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent update.
